### Changed

- The work log under an answer is set in the interface face, one 28px line per call, each led by a glyph of what the call did (a pencil, a page, a prompt) instead of a check mark on a rail.
- Markdown in answers: headings bind to what follows, lists breathe, inline code sits on a quiet fill, quotes hang off a hairline, links underline in their own tint, and tables are bordered cards with a quiet head row.
- The message box: attached files are plain tiles with an ink X on the corner and no "Attached" label, the model and background-task chips have no face or ring, the tools' glyphs are 18px, and tooltips are the page's ink with the surface colour on them.
