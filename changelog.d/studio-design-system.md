### Changed
- A new design system, Studio: the look of Kargul Studio's workflow editor. Inter is the interface
  face (Inter Display for large figures), every glyph is a Tabler icon with filled faces for states
  and identities, and the chrome is a step darker than the content, which sits as a rounded card in
  it on a wide screen. Controls are surfaces (a ring of shadow outside, a line of light inside) on
  shadcn components; Studio is the default theme in dark and light, and every earlier theme stays.
